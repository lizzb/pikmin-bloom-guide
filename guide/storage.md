# Storage management (300 cap, free-to-play)

Researched 2026-11-01.

## Facts (official)

- Starting Pikmin storage is 300; the shop can raise it to 4,500. No free way to expand is documented.
  ([Help Center](https://niantic.helpshift.com/hc/en/23-pikmin-bloom/faq/3040-storage-size-of-each-item/))
- Caps quoted elsewhere (3,000 / 3,200 / 4,000) conflict; check the in-app number.

## Seasonal decor: keep or discard?

Short answer: keep one of each seasonal decor type, release duplicates and common ones.

- Event Pikmin may be useful at a later event, so keep them if you have room.
- Events may not repeat; duplicates of decor unlikely to return (e.g. roadside) are the safest to release.
- Rare decor unlocks only after collecting every color of the regular decor in that category, so don't release a whole category.
- Save leftover seasonal nectar and petals for next year.

## Release order at 300 slots

1. Weak, plain, or duplicate regular Pikmin.
2. Duplicate and common roadside decor.
3. Keep one of each current-month seasonal decor until the month ends.
4. Still full: release the most common seasonal types, not the rare ones.

## Tips

- Keep a few duplicates of the same decor; that's fine.
- Keep favorites: new decor, lifelog memories, special-location decor.
- Hard-to-find decor (restaurants, bakeries, waterfronts) is worth holding.
- Get decor-less Pikmin to 4 hearts and they get decor.
- Rename Pikmin "to release" to defer the decision.
- Feeding rarer nectar before releasing may return better petals.
